## Problem
The two scanners at St. Aurelian export short-axis stacks at 8 mm and 10 mm.

## Impact
Blocking: the preprocessing pipeline can't produce a single input shape, so the MRI baseline can't start.

## Resolution
Plan: resample to 8 mm and add slice thickness as a model input. Tracked by [[TSK-0007|Resample MRI to a common slice thickness]].
